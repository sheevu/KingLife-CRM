# Healthy King LIFE Weight Loss Wellness Center
## Product Requirements Document (PRD)

### 1. Executive Summary
- **Business Name**: Healthy King LIFE Weight Loss Wellness Center
- **Primary Market**: Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh, India (Gomti Nagar, Hazratganj, Aliganj, Indira Nagar, Mahanagar, Ashiyana, Rajajipuram)
- **Primary Type**: Health, weight management, nutrition counselling, fitness programmes, lifestyle coaching, wellness consultation, and customer retention.
- **Core Principle**: This is NOT a generic CRM. It is a customer acquisition, visit-management, WhatsApp automation, wellness journey tracking, and retention platform.

### 2. Complete Customer Lifecycle Funnel
1. **Anonymous Visitor**: Browses services, lands via Google/Meta ads with UTM parameters.
2. **Returning Visitor**: Visits 2+ times before submitting details.
3. **Lead Captured**: Submits phone number or starts onboarding.
4. **Wellness Goal Selected**: Selects target (Weight Management, Nutrition, Fitness, etc.).
5. **WhatsApp Started**: Clicks tracked WhatsApp link (`/wa/...`), initiating conversational flow.
6. **Wellness Evaluation Requested**: Requests comprehensive wellness assessment.
7. **Appointment Scheduled**: Reception or WhatsApp books consultation slot.
8. **Appointment Attended**: Customer checks in at Lucknow center.
9. **Consultation Completed**: Wellness coach reviews evaluation metrics.
10. **Programme Recommended**: Personalized weight/diet plan proposed.
11. **Trial / Intro Session**: First wellness session completed.
12. **Programme Started / Active Customer**: Enrolled in 30/60/90-day program.
13. **Progress Review**: Bi-weekly check on weight, waist, energy, and adherence.
14. **Renewal Due**: 7 days before program end.
15. **Renewed**: Customer extends package or maintenance program.
16. **At Risk**: Attended 3+ times, but missed visits for 7+ days.
17. **Dormant**: Inactive for 21+ days.
18. **Win-back**: Targeted reactivation campaign triggered.
19. **Referral Generated**: Satisfied customer introduces family/friend in Lucknow.

### 3. Key Persona Modes
1. **Public Side**: Lucknow Wellness Landing page, service catalog, 5-step progressive onboarding, appointment booking.
2. **Owner Dashboard**: Strategic metrics (Leads, Appointments, Visits, ₹ Revenue, 92% Retention Rate, CAC/LTV).
3. **Coach Dashboard**: Daily client schedules, at-risk alerts, progress reviews, WhatsApp follow-up tasks.
4. **Reception Dashboard**: Fast check-in/check-out terminal, walk-in registration, missed appointment tracker.
5. **Customer Portal**: My Wellness Journey, visit streaks, progress graphs, meal/workout adherence, coach chat.
6. **WhatsApp Engine**: Webhooks, template dispatch, automated triggers, conversation timeline.
